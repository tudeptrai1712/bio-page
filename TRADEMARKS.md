## 3. Brand & Origin Protection

To prevent user confusion and maintain trust in the ecosystem:

1. **No False Representation of Official Status**:
   - Modified versions, forks, third-party hosting providers, and commercial distributors must **not** present themselves as the official upstream Bio Page project.
   - You must not imply endorsement, affiliation, or sponsorship by the original author (**Hồng Nhật Tú**) or the primary Bio Page repository without explicit written consent.

2. **Renaming Forks and Modified Distributions**:
   - If you distribute a modified version or operate a public SaaS service based on Bio Page, we strongly encourage or recommend choosing a distinctive name (for example, *"Acme Links (powered by Bio Page)"* rather than *"Official Bio Page"*).
   - You may not use domain names or service branding that misleadingly mimics the official upstream project.

3. **Appropriate Attribution**:
   - Downstream forks and deployments should retain author copyright notices as permitted under Section 7 of the AGPLv3.
   - Recommended attribution statements:
     - For unmodified deployments:
       > *"This software is based on Bio Page by Hồng Nhật Tú."*
     - For modified versions or forks:
       > *"This software is a modified version of Bio Page by Hồng Nhật Tú."*
